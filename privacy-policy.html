Privacy Policy for VidyaSetu School ERP & Parent App

Last Updated: November 2026

VidyaSetu ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of school students, parents, teachers, and educational institutions. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards personal and educational information in accordance with educational privacy standards and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Student Academic & Attendance Data: Student names, roll numbers, class/section, daily attendance status, examination marks, and school progress reports.
  • Parent/Guardian Contact Details: Parent names, phone numbers, and email addresses provided during school admission for attendance alerts and communication.
  • Teacher & Faculty Data: Teacher names, contact information, and class assignments.
  • School Records: Homework assignments, school circulars, timetables, and fee payment status.

2. How We Use the Information

  • To provide real-time attendance alerts to verified parents regarding their child's school presence.
  • To allow parents to view academic performance, report cards, and daily homework.
  • To facilitate direct, secure communication between school teachers and parents.
  • To ensure strict tenant isolation—data belonging to one school is never visible or accessible to another school.

3. Data Protection & Child Safety

We take children's privacy seriously:

  • Student data is only accessible to their verified parents/guardians and authorized school staff.
  • We do not sell student, parent, or school data to third parties or advertising networks.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements within the application.
  • All transmissions are protected with secure SSL/TLS encryption.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request the deletion of their accounts or associated data by contacting their school administration or by emailing us at support@vidyasetu.edu.in.
